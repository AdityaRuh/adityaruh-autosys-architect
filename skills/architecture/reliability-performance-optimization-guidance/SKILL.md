# reliability-performance-optimization-guidance

## When to Use

Use this skill when the user asks for hardening, resilience, latency, throughput, or performance improvement guidance for an existing or proposed architecture.

## Procedure

1. Review the proposed or existing architecture and the confirmed performance or reliability requirements.
2. Identify the most likely reliability and performance risks, including single points of failure, saturation points, dependency bottlenecks, and missing observability.
3. Recommend a prioritized checklist of improvements such as redundancy, backpressure handling, caching, queue isolation, timeout and retry strategy, and performance-sensitive storage or compute adjustments.
4. Separate immediate safeguards from later-stage optimizations.
5. Explain the operational and cost impact of the guidance.
6. Keep recommendations within baseline advisory scope and avoid destructive operational actions.

## Pitfalls

- Do not prescribe advanced resilience mechanisms without a confirmed need.
- Do not confuse performance tuning with scale-at-all-costs architecture changes.
- Do not omit observability and failure detection considerations.

## Verification

The skill succeeds when it delivers a prioritized reliability and performance checklist tied to the current architecture and confirmed requirements.
