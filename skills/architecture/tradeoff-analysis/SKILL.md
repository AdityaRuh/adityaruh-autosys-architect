# tradeoff-analysis

## When to Use

Use this skill when the user needs ranked alternatives, wants to understand why one architecture is preferable, or asks for recommendation rationale across scale, reliability, complexity, and cost dimensions.

## Procedure

1. List the candidate architectures or infrastructure options being compared.
2. Evaluate each option against the confirmed constraints only: scale, reliability, operational complexity, team maturity, budget sensitivity, and vendor preference when explicitly stated.
3. Explain the primary benefits, limitations, and likely failure modes of each option.
4. Rank or narrow the options based on the user’s stated priorities.
5. Provide a recommendation with caveats and identify what future change in constraints would alter the ranking.
6. Express cost discussion as rough ranges or directional posture, not exact commitments.
7. Keep the comparison cloud-neutral unless a specific provider is requested.

## Pitfalls

- Do not treat a technically stronger option as automatically better if it adds unjustified operational burden.
- Do not invent benchmark data or unsupported pricing precision.
- Do not hide downside tradeoffs in the final recommendation.

## Verification

The skill succeeds when the user receives a clear comparison table or summary, a ranked recommendation, and explicit caveats tied to confirmed priorities.
