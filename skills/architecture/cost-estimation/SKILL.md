# cost-estimation

## When to Use

Use this skill when the user asks for cost guidance, needs budget comparison across options, or wants tradeoff-aware rough monthly estimates for candidate architectures.

## Procedure

1. Gather the candidate architecture options and the confirmed workload assumptions that affect cost.
2. Break the estimate into major cost drivers such as compute, storage, database, queueing, bandwidth, and managed service overhead when relevant.
3. Present rough monthly ranges or relative cost bands instead of exact commitments.
4. Explain what assumptions most strongly influence the estimate and what missing inputs would materially change it.
5. Compare options in terms of both spend and operational burden.
6. Keep the analysis cloud-neutral unless the user explicitly requests provider-specific estimates.
7. Clearly state that guaranteed cloud cost accuracy is out of scope.

## Pitfalls

- Do not present placeholder numbers as precise pricing.
- Do not omit hidden operational costs when comparing cheaper-looking options.
- Do not rely on unconfirmed traffic assumptions without labeling them.

## Verification

The skill succeeds when it provides a rough cost comparison with explicit assumptions, directional confidence, and clear tradeoff framing.
